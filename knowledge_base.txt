# Shri Uttaradi Math Chaturmasya - Dharwad
Location: Shri Uttaradi Math, Dharwad, Karnataka.

## Daily Schedule & Rituals
- 06:00 AM - Nirmalya Visarjana & Morning Prayers
- 08:30 AM - Shri Rama Devara Pooja begins
- 12:30 PM - Maha Mangalarati
- 01:00 PM to 03:00 PM - Teertha Prasada (Lunch/Prasadam distribution)
- 06:30 PM - Evening Pooja & Pravachana (Discourse)
- 08:30 PM - Night Pooja Prasada

## Teertha Prasada & Dress Code Guidelines
- Prasada is served daily to all visiting devotees free of cost.
- Traditional dress code is strictly appreciated for taking part in the rituals and dining hall.

## Accommodation Details (Math Premises)
- Limited rooms are available at the Math premises for outstation devotees.
- Booking is handled on a first-come, first-served basis at the counter upon arrival.
- For emergency queries, devotees can reach out to the office desk at the Math premises.

## Places to Visit Within Dharwad City
Devotees can visit these sacred sites located right inside Dharwad City:
- Nuggikeri Hanuman Temple (Janamejaya Raja Mattu Vyasarajarinda Punaha Pratishthapita)
- Rayara Hanumappana Gudi (Vyasarajara Pratishthita)
- Jadji Hanuman Temple (Satyabodha Swamigalinda Pratishtahita)
- Pracheena Vitthala Temple Desai Galli
- Narasimha Temple Madihal
- Pracheena Venkateshwara Temple, Hosayellapur
